Atlas and Metal reliability audit

Atlas is the control plane that places and manages customer virtual machines (VMs). Metal (metald) is the daemon on each bare-metal host that runs the VMs. This page records what breaks when a part of the system fails, what the system does then, and which fallbacks are missing.

The audit has two parts. First, a review of the Atlas and Metal source code for failure paths. Second, fault injection on development hosts: the audit stops services, fills disks, and cuts the network, then records what the system does. Each result goes into the scenario table below. The log at the end is append-only and shows progress.
