Frappe Cloud V2 launch plan

Engineering plan · 4 October 2026

Frappe Cloud V2 has three parts: Central (teams, billing, and resources), Atlas (virtual machines, or VMs), and Pilot (Frappe benches, apps, and sites). This plan lists the engineering work that must be done before the first public V2 offer.

Public launch does not turn off V1. Customers stay on V1 until their path to V2 works. That move is a separate project, listed near the end.

Size: S = under 1 week · M = 1–3 weeks · L = 1–2 months · XL = more than 2 months.

Priority: P0 = data, access, or money risk · P1 = needed for a good launch.

IDs: L-numbers come from the full delivery plan. Other IDs are new tasks added after the plan audit.

Milestones

Do the milestones in this order. A task can start early when its dependencies are done.

  1. Stop the risks. Fix access checks, backup choice, and backup status. Start site move, because it is the largest task.
  2. Make data and money safe. Restore, archive, billing correctness, invoices, and deploy checks.
  3. Complete the journey. Site creation choices, trial, site move, support access, and incidents.
  4. Prove it. Restore drills, payment gateway tests, region tests, and the launch check.

Not for launch

Do not advertise these features at launch.

  • MariaDB encryption at rest
  • Point-in-time recovery
  • DB replicas, failover, and active-active hosting
  • Customer API and webhooks
  • Paid marketplace apps
  • GitLab, Codeberg, and monorepo apps
  • Site transfer between teams
  • Customer backup storage and backup region
  • Masked staging copy and table-level DB access
  • Annual prepayment and pre-billing estimates
  • PayPal, M-Pesa, and other regional payment methods
  • Static egress IP, private network, wildcard domains, and IPv6
  • Scheduled auto-update, site DB users, and bulk site drop

Launch check

Launch when all of these are true:

  1. Every launch task has a result that its owner approved.
  2. The paid and trial journeys pass end to end.
  3. The archive period, plans, and regions are published.
  4. Any advertised Freemium or partner path passes its own tests.

Sources

This plan is a short version of an internal delivery plan. That plan used a review of the Frappe Cloud V1 code (frappe/press, frappe/agent), the V2 code, open Press issues, and six months of support tickets. Sizes are estimates, not a schedule.
