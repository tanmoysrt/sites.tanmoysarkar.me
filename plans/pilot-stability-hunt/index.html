Pilot stability hunt

Live status of a bug hunt on Pilot. Updated 2026-10-14 23:17 UTC. 12 log entries, newest first.

PlanLog

Goal

Make Pilot stable, correct and light on resources for production use. Find bugs on a real VM, fix the small ones, and record every finding. Test with Frappe version-16 and develop. Bug fixes only: no new features unless a bug needs one.

Ground rules

  • Base: latest develop, local branch fix/bughunt. Nothing is committed or pushed.
  • Test host: one Ubuntu 24.04 VM (1 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, no swap at start). It may be resized up and down to test tuning. Disk stays the same.
  • Domains: *.ip-<a-b-c-d>.swiftwave.xyz wildcard DNS, plus the Atlas auto router for the domain-provider TLS tests.
  • Every finding gets a log entry: what failed, the root cause, the fix (or proposal) and how it was checked.

  • 2026-10-15 FINDING (db tuning) MariaDB is sized only when it is first provisioned and on a Central host's first boot (admin/backend/central_bootstrap.py main -> tune_database). A VM resize keeps the old my.cnf and unit MemoryHigh/MemoryMax. Downsize 8 -> 4 GB keeps MemoryMax 3027M (75% of RAM) and a 1.5 GB pool. Upsize keeps the small pool, and the Admin UI then accepts a pool larger than the stale cgroup MemoryMax (UI limits read live RAM). Press re-sizes after its resize job (adjust_memory_config from resize_server.py); Pilot runs inside the VM and must detect the change at boot.
